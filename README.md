# small-business-network
In this lab, we are going to set up a template of a typical small business network.

draft notes:
1. The business will have a mix of wired desktops, printer/copy machine, a file server and laptops connected to the network by wireless. They will use a router, firewall and switches.
2. The office network will be segregated into several VLANs for each department.
3. The departments in the network
   1. CEO Office 
   2. Accounting
   3. Sales 
   4. Marketing
   5. IT
   6. HR
4.
5. VLAN assignments
   1.  VLAN 10 SALES
   2.  VLAN 20 MARKETING
   3.  VLAN 30 HR
   4.  VLAN 40 ACC
   5.  VLAN 50 SERVERS
   6.  VLAN 90 (IOT) - Printers
   7.  VLAN 60 CEO's Office
   8.  VLAN 70 IT
6. Security:
   1.   All unused ports are disabled/secured
   2.   All open ports are tied to device MAC address
